# Initial Project Discussion & Snapshot Preparation

**Project:** ASAP - Automated Social-media Aggregation Platform  
**Course:** ECE496 Capstone  
**Supervisor:** Prof. Hans-Arno Jacobsen  
**Team:** Tianyu Ling, Shengya Huang, Molin Zhang, Zhengfei Yu

## Purpose

Establish the team's initial understanding of ASAP, prepare the ECE496 Project Snapshot, and identify questions that needed clarification before beginning implementation.

## Discussion / Progress

The team developed an initial understanding of ASAP as an open-source replacement for the third-party social-media feed currently used on the MSRG website.

The initial project concept was to build an extensible platform that:

- retrieves posts from LinkedIn, X, and Bluesky;
- converts provider-specific content into a common representation;
- filters and curates content for the MSRG feed;
- displays the resulting feed on the MSRG website;
- keeps provider-specific components modular so additional platforms can be added later; and
- includes testing, deployment, and documentation support.

During Snapshot preparation, the team identified questions concerning:

- limitations and pain points of the current solution;
- expected technical depth and major capstone challenges;
- future project scope;
- social-media API access and credentials;
- post ordering and prioritization;
- content representation and display;
- hosting/server availability; and
- whether ASAP should only ingest MSRG-owned accounts or also discover external posts.

## Decisions / Outcomes

- The Project Snapshot was completed and submitted.
- The team's high-level understanding of ASAP was established.
- The questions raised during Snapshot preparation were subsequently discussed and clarified.
- The team moved forward to the formal project kickoff stage.
